How it works

  1. Open the builder link above (it opens in a new tab — keep this page open too).
  2. In the left sidebar, click the molecule or ion you're building first.
  3. Drag atoms from the bar above the canvas onto the blank workspace.
  4. Hover an atom to reveal a small blue dot — drag it to another atom to draw a bond. Click an existing bond to cycle single → double → triple → none.
  5. Drag lone pair (••) tokens onto atoms that need them. Hover a placed lone pair and click the small ↻ button to rotate it horizontal or vertical.
  6. Drag + / − charge tokens onto any atom carrying a formal charge.
  7. Once your structure looks right, check the “Quiz answer” box under the canvas — it updates automatically. Make sure there's no orange warning about unattached pieces, then click Copy text.
  8. Switch to the quiz tab and paste the copied text into the matching question. Repeat for every molecule and ion in the list below.
💡 Tip: the quiz grades your answer as exact text, so always use Copy text from the builder rather than retyping it — a single missing colon or bracket will mark it wrong.
